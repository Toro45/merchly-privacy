What Happens When You Request Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA), you may permanently delete your Merchly merchant account and all associated records at any time.

Submitting this request initiates the permanent purging of:

  • Account Profile & Credentials: Your user identity, email, authentication tokens, and profile settings in Supabase Auth.
  • Storefront Configuration: Store names, handles, subdomains, custom domains, and theme customizations.
  • Catalog & Inventory: All physical products, digital download files, course curricula, variants, and stock counts.
  • Hosted Binary Assets: High-resolution media, banners, digital files, and video lessons hosted on Bunny.net Edge Object Storage.
  • Order & Transaction History: Past customer receipts, fulfillment records, and customer communications.

Note: Anonymized financial settlement metadata required for statutory tax and accounting audits may be retained for the minimum legally mandated retention period.
