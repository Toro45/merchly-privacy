Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 | Last Updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Merchly ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide commerce infrastructure, storefront hosting, and mobile merchant management applications across Android, Web, and Progressive Web Applications (PWA).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, web storefronts, and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). By creating an account or accessing our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and optimize our merchant and storefront services:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, merchant name, store identifier, and authentication credentials through our secure authentication provider (Supabase Auth).
  • Store & Product Data: We store merchant-submitted product titles, descriptions, pricing, inventory quantities, variant configurations, digital download attributes, and course curricula.
  • Digital Assets & Media: Product images, promotional banners, downloadable digital documents, and course materials uploaded by merchants are securely stored and delivered via edge object storage.
  • Customer & Transaction Records: When customers place orders through your storefront, we process customer contact details, itemized order specifications, and delivery addresses necessary for fulfillment.
  • Device & Network Information: We may collect standard technical data, such as IP addresses, operating system version, and browser/app identifiers to ensure optimal caching and offline synchronization.

3. Payment Processing & Financial Information

Merchly never collects, stores, or processes credit card numbers, CVVs, or sensitive banking details directly on our servers or device applications.

All financial checkout transactions are handled through certified third-party payment gateways (including Paystack, PayPal, and supported bank transfer settlement processors). When making a payment, buyers interact directly with the payment provider's encrypted tokenization endpoint.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business operations and application functionality, including:

  • Authenticating merchants and providing private, tenant-isolated merchant dashboard controls.
  • Rendering customized, live storefronts across Web and native Android applications.
  • Delivering itemized order confirmation and fulfillment emails to customers.
  • Accurately tracking Cloud Edge Storage consumption against merchant allocations.
  • Enforcing security policies, preventing unauthorized access, and detecting fraudulent activities.

5. Data Storage, Security & Third-Party Service Providers

We take data security seriously. All network transmissions are protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We partner with reputable cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: For relational database persistence, user authentication, and row-level security (RLS) enforcement.
  • Bunny.net: For high-performance, globally distributed edge object storage and CDN asset delivery.
  • Resend / Edge Email Handlers: For automated transactional notification delivery.

We strictly enforce multi-tenant isolation, ensuring that each merchant's database records and assets remain inaccessible to unauthorized parties.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal and store information for as long as your account is active or as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Account Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account, catalog, and associated personal data at any time. You can initiate account deletion through the Merchant Settings panel or by emailing privacy@merchlystore.com. Upon verification, all associated store records and uploaded digital assets will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is intended for individuals aged 18 and older, or those operating legal commercial enterprises with appropriate adult consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such data immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect architectural enhancements or regulatory changes. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and by posting an in-app notice when appropriate.
